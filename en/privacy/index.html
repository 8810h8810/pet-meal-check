Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Fed Yet? handles your data.

Saving feeding checks

The dog and cat apps save checks in your browser's local storage. In device-only mode, feeding checks are not sent to our server.

Optional family sharing

Only when you choose to share with family or join through an invitation do we send and store checks, a shared chart identifier, and an anonymous user identifier in Google Firebase. No name or email address is required. Connections to Firebase also involve connection information such as your IP address.

Anyone with an invitation link can join, view, and change that chart. Share it only with your family, and do not post it publicly. Anonymous authentication and database access rules control participation. The operator can manage stored data through the Firebase administration console.

Changes made offline are saved on your device and sent after reconnection. If changes to the same box overlap, the record shared first is kept. “Stop sharing on this device” disconnects this device; it does not delete your family's chart or the server records.

Clearing browser data may remove local checks and anonymous authentication information. Use your family's invitation link to rejoin.

Personal information

There is currently no account registration requiring your name, email address, or postal address.

Analytics

Google Analytics loads only after you review and explicitly allow this version of analytics in this browser. Permission from an earlier version is not carried over automatically. Your choice is saved for 180 days and can be changed at any time in Analytics settings below.

We measure the first time in each page view that you check a previously unchecked box and successfully save it on your device (record_saved). If this browser also saved on an earlier day and you open the page and save on a later day, we also measure a later-day return save (record_return_saved). The only additional category is dog or cat. These are browser-usage measures, not counts of people or meals, or confirmation that every family device has synced.

We do not send check contents, weekdays or morning/night choices, health information, pet names, conversation text, invitation links, shared-chart IDs, Firebase user IDs, or authentication tokens to analytics. We do not attach our own user IDs or record dates and times to the new events.

To recognize a later-day return, we store only the calendar date of the last successful save after consent in this browser, separately for dog and cat. We use that date for at most 180 days and only while consent is valid, deleting expired data at the next check. We also delete it when analytics is turned off. The date itself is not sent to analytics, and we do not inspect or send records made before consent.

Google Analytics itself also processes pseudonymous cookie IDs, connection information, and normal receipt times, so this measurement is not fully anonymous. Turning analytics off disables it, clears existing analytics cookies and the local return-date marker, and reloads the page if the tag was loaded. Recording and family sharing work if you decline.

Measured URLs exclude queries and fragments. A public campaign’s source, medium, and name may be sent as separate categories only when they exactly match a fixed, preregistered combination. Arbitrary input values and person-specific identifiers are not allowed. No campaign combinations are currently registered.

Advertising

We may introduce Google AdSense advertising in the future. Google and other advertising providers may use cookies and information about previous visits to this and other websites to display ads. Third parties may also use web beacons and IP addresses to collect information in connection with advertising.

You can manage personalized ads through Google's ad settings. See how Google uses data from partner sites and apps.

Home Screen invitation cookie

Choosing “Join and add to Home Screen” saves invitation information in a cookie on this site for up to 24 hours. It helps a newly added Home Screen app join the same chart. The app deletes its cookie after successfully joining. This cookie contains no authentication token or history of feeding changes. Previously installed apps may need the invitation link pasted once.

Changes

We may update this policy as features and services change. If advertising is introduced, we will take the required steps under applicable rules and provider policies.
